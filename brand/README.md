# Brand assets

- `logo-mark.svg` — square mark for avatars and app icons.
- `wordmark.svg` — horizontal logo and wordmark.
- `social-card.svg` — 1200×630 social preview.

Use the mark on dark surfaces, keep clear space of at least one keyhole width,
and do not recolor the gradient or place the mark on low-contrast backgrounds.
The canonical colors are emerald `#10b981`, teal `#0d9488`, ink `#0b0f0d`, and
mint text `#6ee7b7`.
